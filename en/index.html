Data, Analytics, BI, AI

Read, structure and make data useful

We explore how to turn a business need into reliable information, then into a decision or an action. Rather than adding yet another technical tutorial about one tool or another to the thousands already on YouTube, our aim is different: bring Data closer to the real world, understand the purpose of each feature and the need it actually serves.

At a time when agility should help us move faster, noise, tool overload and passing trends still too often distract us from the essential question: why are we doing this? This site brings together applied Data, BI and AI demonstrations, explained concretely and without gimmicks.
